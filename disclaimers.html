SECTION 1 Electrostatic Discharge (ESD) & Silicon Handling Guidelines

Semiconductor devices, including microcontrollers (MCUs), Field-Programmable Gate Arrays (FPGAs), operational amplifiers, MOSFETs, and CMOS logic gates, are inherently susceptible to catastrophic or latent damage caused by Electrostatic Discharge (ESD). Human touch can carry electrical potentials in excess of 15,000 Volts, which easily ruptures gate oxide dielectrics rated for 3.3V or 5V operation.

  • Mandatory Grounding: All items must be handled exclusively in an ESD-protected area (EPA) complying with ANSI/ESD S20.20 or IEC 61340-5-1 standards.
  • Protective Gear: Operators must wear continuous monitoring wrist grounding straps and conductive heel straps on dissipative flooring.
  • Packaging Integrity: Components shipped in antistatic bags, conductive carrier tubes, or tape-and-reel packages must not be opened in unshielded environments.
  • Damage Exclusion: Latent semiconductor failure caused by ungrounded handling will void all refund claims and factory warranties.

SECTION 2 Absolute Maximum Electrical Ratings & Polarity Precautions

Operating components beyond the "Absolute Maximum Ratings" listed in manufacturer datasheets—even momentarily—may cause permanent damage, internal bond wire melting, or thermal runaway.

  • Reverse Polarity: Unless an explicit reverse-polarity protection diode or P-channel MOSFET is present, applying inverted DC voltage to VCC/GND terminals will instantly destroy the IC.
  • Over-Voltage & Inductive Spikes: Driving inductive loads (solenoids, DC motors, relays) without appropriate flyback/freewheeling diodes will generate back-EMF spikes that exceed silicon breakdown thresholds.
  • Logic Level Translation: Connecting 5V logic signals directly to 3.3V-only GPIO pins (such as ESP32, STM32, or Raspberry Pi RP2040) without bidirectional logic level shifters constitutes user error.

SECTION 3 Educational STEM & DIY Assembly Kits Soldering Advisory

Our DIY kits (including oscilloscopes, soldering stations, robot chassis, and sensor labs) require active assembly, mechanical fastener installation, and manual soldering by the purchaser or end user.

  • Thermal Overheating: Excessive dwell time with a soldering iron (greater than 3 seconds at >350°C) will lift copper PCB pads and destroy heat-sensitive SMD transistors or electrolytic capacitor seals.
  • Solder Bridges & Short Circuits: Users are solely responsible for conducting pre-power visual inspections and multimeter continuity checks to prevent catastrophic short circuits between power rails.
  • Ventilation & Fume Extraction: Rosin-core solder and flux release airborne irritants. Always operate in well-ventilated spaces with activated carbon fume extraction units.

SECTION 4 High-Voltage Hazards & Lithium-Ion / LiPo Battery Handling

Certain kits and power modules interact with high-voltage mains AC or high-energy density secondary Lithium-Polymer (LiPo) / Lithium-Ion (18650 / 21700) battery cells.

  • Mains Voltage Lethality: Any circuit operating above 48V DC or 30V AC RMS presents a risk of severe electric shock, electrocution, and fire. Only certified electricians or licensed engineers may interface with mains electricity.
  • Battery Fire & Explosion Risk: Lithium cells must always be paired with a dedicated Battery Management System (BMS) with over-charge, over-discharge, and over-current protection. Never puncture, crush, or solder directly to battery terminals.
  • UN 38.3 Shipping Mandates: Components shipped with battery cells comply with IATA DGR and DOT hazmat transport regulations.

SECTION 5 Warranty Policy: Unopened Silicon vs. Soldered / Modified Parts

Electronix stands behind 100% genuine component authenticity. However, because semiconductor reliability is heavily influenced by the customer's assembly process, strict RMA criteria apply:

  • Return Window: Unopened, unprogrammed, and unsoldered components in original manufacturer sealed ESD moisture barrier packaging may be returned within 30 days of delivery, subject to a 15% restocking fee for inspection.
  • Soldered Silicon Exception: Once a component has been soldered, socketed, lead-trimmed, or energized, NO RETURNS OR REFUNDS CAN BE ACCEPTED, unless an inherent manufacturing silicon flaw is proven via independent decapsulation analysis.
  • Pre-Assembly Verification: We strongly urge customers to perform cold resistance testing and bench breadboard tests with appropriate IC sockets prior to permanent PCB soldering.

SECTION 6 Exclusion of Use in Life-Critical, Medical & Military Applications

Components and kits distributed by Electronix are standard commercial and industrial grade (unless explicitly designated with MIL-STD-883 or AEC-Q100 automotive qualification in writing).

  • No Medical / Life-Support Authorization: These products are NOT authorized for use in critical surgical implants, life-support machines, nuclear facilities, avionics flight control, or missile guidance systems where failure could cause injury or loss of life.
  • Indemnification: Any buyer incorporating our components into mission-critical applications agrees to indemnify and hold harmless Electronix from all third-party liability, damages, and legal expenses.

SECTION 7 Environmental & Electromagnetic Regulatory Compliance

We adhere strictly to international consumer, industrial, and environmental directives:

  • RoHS 3 (EU Directive 2015/863): All stocked components are certified lead-free (<0.1% Pb) and free of restricted phthalates, cadmium, and polybrominated biphenyls.
  • REACH (EC 1907/2006): Products do not contain Substances of Very High Concern (SVHC) exceeding 0.1% by weight.
  • FCC Part 15 / CE RED: Pre-assembled modules featuring RF radios (Wi-Fi, BLE, LoRa) conform to modular emission standards. However, the final composite product assembled by the customer must undergo independent certification before commercial sale.
  • WEEE Directive: Electronic waste must not be disposed of in municipal trash. Recycle at certified e-waste facilities.

SECTION 8 Export Control Regulations (EAR99 / Dual-Use Provisions)

Certain advanced microcontrollers, high-speed DSPs, and cryptographic security accelerators are subject to the United States Export Administration Regulations (EAR) and European Dual-Use regulations.

  • Customers agree not to export, re-export, or divert any silicon to embargoed nations or prohibited entities listed on the U.S. Consolidated Screening List.
  • Customers represent that components will not be used in weapons of mass destruction, chemical/biological delivery systems, or unauthorized military hardware.
