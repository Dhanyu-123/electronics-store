THE ELECTRONIX STORY

Why We Started

Too often, engineering students and young makers are priced out of high-tech innovation because local retailers mark up basic microcontrollers, sensor breakouts, and breadboard parts by 300% to 500%.

We eliminated the middlemen. By procuring directly from certified semiconductor fabrication distributors and optimizing our logistics from Bangalore, we offer genuine components starting at just ₹28 (TP4056 USB-C charger) and ₹165 (Arduino Nano Type-C).

ENGINEERING STANDARDS

Quality & Inspection Lab

Our metrology facility rigorously verifies silicon integrity before any product enters stock.

🔍

Laser Decapsulation & Die Inspection

Random batch sampling verifies manufacturer die markings, bond wire integrity, and package mold compounds to eliminate recycled or remarked silicon.

⚡

ANSI/ESD S20.20 Cleanroom Standards

Our inspection benches feature dissipative mats, wrist straps connected to earth ground, and constant ionization fans ensuring zero static discharge damage.

📊

Full Parametric Electrical Testing

Microcontroller oscillators, GPIO voltage tolerances, and buck regulator efficiency curves are benchmarked using digital oscilloscopes and logic analyzers.
