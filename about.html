METROLOGY & INSPECTION LAB

Silicon Integrity & Quality Engineering

In an era of supply chain vulnerabilities and cloned integrated circuits, Electronix enforces rigorous verification protocols before any silicon leaves our warehouse.

• Optical Topography & Decapsulation

High-magnification stereoscopic optical microscopes inspect laser mark typography, lead-frame plating uniformity, and die markings to detect sanded or remarketed ICs.

• Parametric Curve Tracing

Automated curve tracers verify ESD protection diode drops, pin leakage currents, and transistor breakdown voltages against manufacturer factory specifications.

• ANSI/ESD S20.20 Certified Handling

Dissipative ESD flooring, constant ground monitoring, and low-charging carrier tapes protect against sub-threshold gate dielectric punctures.

• Moisture Barrier Sealed (MSL Levels 1-6)

SMD ICs subject to JEDEC J-STD-033 moisture sensitivity levels are vacuum sealed with desiccant and cobalt-free humidity indicator cards before dispatch.
