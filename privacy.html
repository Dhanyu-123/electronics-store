Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 • Last Updated: September 25, 2026

🛡️ Zero-Spam & Data Integrity Commitment

At Electronix, we respect your privacy. We never sell, rent, or trade your contact information, component inquiries, or RFQ specs to any third-party advertisers. All data is solely used to service your orders and technical requests.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information strictly when you voluntarily submit it to us through our web forms or direct email inquiries:

  • Contact Information: Full name, institutional/company affiliation, email address, phone number, and delivery city/pincode.
  • Component RFQ Data: Target component part numbers, bill of materials (BOM), required quantities, target delivery schedules, and technical consultation requests.
  • Technical Feedback: Inquiries regarding pinouts, firmware compatibility, or lab testing certifications.

2. How We Use Your Information

The data gathered is utilized exclusively to:

  • Process and generate competitive quotation sheets in Indian Rupees (₹) for requested electronic silicon and DIY kits.
  • Verify delivery feasibility, transit logistics, and anti-static packaging requirements.
  • Communicate order status, tracking consignment numbers, and technical datasheets directly to your email.
  • Prevent fraudulent activities, spam bots, and unauthorized access to our RFQ infrastructure.

3. Web Analytics & Cookies

We utilize lightweight, privacy-conscious telemetry to optimize user navigation and ensure catalog accessibility:

  • Google Analytics 4 (GA4): Aggregated, anonymized metrics (such as page views, popular component categories, and browser screen resolutions) with IP anonymization enabled.
  • Microsoft Clarity: Visual heatmaps and anonymous session replays to identify UI bugs and improve button responsiveness without recording keystrokes on sensitive form fields.

You can disable cookies at any time via your browser settings without affecting your ability to browse our catalog or submit RFQ requests.

4. Data Protection & Indian DPDP Act Compliance

In accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000:

  • Right to Access & Correction: You may request a summary of the personal data we hold or request corrections to your contact details at any time.
  • Right to Erasure: Upon completing your order or inquiry, you can request full erasure of your contact details from our inquiry log.
  • Data Security: All form transmissions are encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3/HTTPS).

5. Contact Our Privacy Officer

If you have any questions, data deletion requests, or concerns regarding your privacy, please email our team directly:
